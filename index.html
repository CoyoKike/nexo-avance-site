Services

How we help

Three ways we help, backed by deep experience in strategy, operations, compliance and external affairs.

Leadership training & professional development

Practical programs that help managers and emerging leaders lead teams, manage change and deliver results.

  • Leadership development for new and mid-level managers
  • Supervisory skills, coaching and accountability
  • Workshops and retreats for leadership teams
  • Staff training on compliance, quality and program integrity

For organizations growing their bench of leaders or preparing for succession.

Fractional executive leadership

Interim or part-time leadership when you're between leaders or short-staffed, without the cost of a full-time hire.

  • Interim or fractional COO, CPO and program executive roles
  • Operations, program and compliance strategy
  • Grants, contracts and funder reporting oversight
  • Data, performance measurement and quality improvement

For organizations in transition, scaling up, or filling a key leadership gap.

Nonprofits, faith-based organizations & ministries

Streamlined programs and structure for growth, so your mission can reach more people.

  • Program design and streamlining
  • Organizational structure, roles and policies
  • Planning for growth and sustainability
  • Leadership and volunteer development

For congregations, ministries and community organizations ready to grow with structure.

About

Gina Hudson

Principal, Nexo Avance Strategic Advisory

Gina is a nonprofit and human services executive who has led large, complex programs from the inside. Most recently she served as Chief Program and Compliance Officer at a regional elder services agency with an annual budget of about $100 million, leading home care, protective services and long-term services and supports through a team of directors and senior managers.

She founded Nexo Avance to bring that experience to organizations that need steady, hands-on leadership and want to grow the next generation of leaders on their own teams.
